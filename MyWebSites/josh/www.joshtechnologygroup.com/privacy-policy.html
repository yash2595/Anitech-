Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may request certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Email address

  • Address, City, State, ZIP/Postal code
  • Usage Data

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically and may include IP address, browser type/version, visited pages, time and date of visits, device identifiers, and diagnostic data.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on Our Service and store information. This includes:

  • Cookies or Browser Cookies: Small files stored on Your device. You can disable them in your browser settings, but some features may not work without Cookies.
  • Flash Cookies: Stored objects used for saving preferences.
  • Web Beacons: Small files in emails or web pages to track views and interactions.

Cookies can be Session (deleted when browser closes) or Persistent (stored longer). We use both to improve user experience.
